7. Routes, ETAs, GPS, maps, and ERP estimates

Routes, route geometry, ETAs, traffic assumptions, GPS readings, place results, road matches, map positions, closures, restrictions, and road conditions can be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, or stale. Route providers can return different results for the same request. You must assess the route against current conditions.

ERP is an estimate, not an official charge

LTA or the relevant authority controls ERP gantries, operating rules, rates, exemptions, and actual charges. Toll Map uses route geometry, estimated crossing time, vehicle class, and available public data to calculate an informational estimate. A rate can change when a gantry is crossed even one minute earlier or later, and actual arrival time can differ from the estimate. Data updates, clock differences, detours, direction matching, traffic, route changes, or an authority transition can also change the result.

Toll Map does not guarantee that an estimated toll is complete, current, or the amount you will pay. It is not an invoice, official toll statement, or substitute for checking official ERP information.
