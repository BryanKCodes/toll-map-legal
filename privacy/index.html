1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Toll Map mobile application, its route-planning backend, purchase and export services, and this legal website (together, the “Service”). Toll Map is a Singapore-focused route-comparison and ERP-estimation aid. It is not operated by or affiliated with the Land Transport Authority of Singapore (“LTA”), Google, OpenStreetMap, Valhalla, Apple, Google Play, AdMob, or RevenueCat.

The operator identified above is intended to be the organisation responsible for personal data processed for Toll Map. That identity must be completed before launch. Third-party providers may separately determine how they process information under their own privacy notices.

If we make a material change, we intend to update the version and effective date and provide reasonable notice in the app or through another appropriate channel before the change takes effect, where required. Continued use after an effective change will be handled subject to applicable law; a new consent will be sought when one is required.

2. A short summary

  • Toll Map does not ask you to create an account with a name, email address, or password. It creates an installation-level, pseudonymous principal identifier and credential.
  • Route planning can involve precise or approximate device location, selected origins and destinations, place searches, timing and vehicle information, and route results.
  • Google Maps Platform, the self-hosted Valhalla routing engine, LTA-derived information, and OpenStreetMap data support map, routing, place, road, and ERP features.
  • Free users may receive Google Mobile Ads. Advertising behavior depends on consent choices, device settings, region, and applicable law. Premium users currently bypass Toll Map's AdMob initialisation and ad placements after their entitlement is resolved as active.
  • Apple or Google handles store payment; RevenueCat helps manage the pseudonymous purchase entitlement. Toll Map's backend receives a minimised purchase and entitlement record.
  • This legal website contains no Toll Map analytics, ad scripts, cookies, or other tracking code. GitHub hosts the site and may process ordinary network and security logs.

5. Device permissions and local storage

Toll Map requests foreground location access. You can decline or later change the permission in device settings, but current-location features will not work without it. The current app does not request background-location permission. Your device and map SDK may determine whether the location is precise or approximate.

The app stores its bearer credential, principal ID, and RevenueCat app-user ID in Expo SecureStore. Saved Home, Work, and Activity places are also stored in SecureStore; recent searches are stored in app-local AsyncStorage, currently up to 15 items. You can delete saved or recent places through the app where that control is provided. Removing the app or clearing its data is controlled by the operating system and may not delete server-side purchase, security, or entitlement records.

6. Advertising, consent, and tracking choices

Free users may see Google Mobile Ads native placements integrated among route-option cards and may choose to watch a rewarded ad before exporting certain Valhalla/custom-generated routes to Google Maps. Current Google-provided baseline and alternative routes are intended to remain directly exportable. Once an active Premium entitlement is resolved, the current app bypasses Toll Map's AdMob initialisation and placements.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (“UMP”) to obtain and update consent information and to make an Ad privacy choices entry point available when required. Available choices and the resulting ad-serving mode vary with region, age treatment, applicable law, consent, and Google configuration. Ads may be personalised, non-personalised, or limited; we do not promise one mode for every user.

On iOS, Toll Map may request App Tracking Transparency (“ATT”) permission where applicable. Declining ATT prevents access to Apple's advertising identifier for tracking, but does not necessarily prevent ads, contextual processing, fraud prevention, security, measurement allowed by platform rules, or processing needed to provide the Service. Declining optional advertising consent similarly does not stop processing that is necessary for security, requested features, legal duties, or consent management.

For rewarded exports, Google server-side verification receives a pseudonymous principal identifier and route-bound verification data so the backend can validate the reward. That data should not be publicly logged or used as a human-readable route example.

You can revisit available Google consent choices using Settings → Ad privacy choices, change ATT in iOS Settings, and change location permission in device settings. Premium removes Toll Map's in-app ad placements, but does not erase earlier ad, purchase, security, or consent records.

10. Security

Current safeguards include device secure storage for bearer credentials and key saved-place data, storage of a credential digest rather than the plaintext bearer token on the backend, authenticated backend requests, rotatable credentials, short-lived route-bound export authorisations, single-use permit checks, constrained telemetry fields, and minimised RevenueCat webhook records.

No transmission, device, database, or service can be guaranteed perfectly secure. The operator must complete production access-control, encryption, secrets, logging, backup, incident-response, vendor, and breach-notification controls before launch. If a data breach is notifiable under Singapore law, the operator will follow applicable assessment and notification duties.

11. Your rights and choices

Subject to the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 and other applicable law, you may ask for access to personal data held about you and information about its use or disclosure, ask us to correct an error or omission, withdraw consent for future collection/use/disclosure where consent is the basis, and request deletion or cessation where legally available. You may also complain about how your information is handled.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully carried out. We will explain likely consequences; a feature may stop working if the information is reasonably needed to provide it. Some processing may continue without consent where law permits or requires it, including security, fraud prevention, legal claims, and required records.

To make a request, contact [DPO/privacy contact]. Include the app's Support ID from Settings if available, the request you are making, and enough information to verify that the record belongs to your installation. Do not send bearer credentials, full payment details, precise route history, or identity documents unless we provide a secure method and they are genuinely required. We may ask for proportionate verification, respond within applicable timeframes, and explain any lawful refusal or fee.

You may first raise a concern with the operator. You can also learn about complaint routes from Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission.

12. Children

Toll Map is not directed to children under 13. A person below the age of majority should use it only with a parent or guardian's review and permission. If the operator learns that a child's personal data was collected contrary to this policy, it will investigate and take appropriate deletion or restriction steps.

13. External destinations and third-party terms
